# Problem 10: Design improvements

- Added subtle product-card entrance motion and reduced-motion support, making browsing feel polished without distracting shoppers.
- Added a gentle product-image zoom on hover, making cards feel interactive and encouraging product exploration.
- Cleaned up visible encoding artifacts and standardized displayed punctuation for a more professional storefront.

Together, these details make the shop feel more trustworthy and finished, which can keep shoppers browsing longer and increase the chance they click through to product pages and convert.

- Added a timed chat-widget entrance: it appears two seconds after load, stays visible for four seconds, and slides away. This creates a gentle discovery moment without covering the storefront immediately.

- Added the Yale-sweater bulldog mascot as a timed visual: it rises from the bottom after two seconds, stays visible for four seconds, and descends out of sight. The playful school-spirit moment strengthens brand personality and can make the storefront more memorable without interrupting shopping.

- Refined the mascot animation to restart on every page navigation, wait 1.3 seconds, slide up from below the window, remain visible for four seconds, and use a different bottom position on each route.
